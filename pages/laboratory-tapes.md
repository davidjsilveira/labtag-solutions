![Lab Tapes and Cryogenic Tapes](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/TXS-25-use2.jpg "/media/gatorcut/TXS-25-use2.jpg")

Tape is the ID you use when the vial, rack, or tray does not need a printed barcode.

LabTAG cryogenic, freezer, autoclave, and general-use tapes color-code samples and equipment. CryoSTUCK® tape sticks to already-frozen plastic. C-Kur™ seals tear if someone opens the box. GatorCUT™ is not a second catalog—it is the pocket roll that tears to length without scissors or a dispenser, on cryo, deep-freeze, autoclave, and static-cling tapes.

## Dewars, metal, and already-frozen plastic

![Permanent cryogenic GatorCUT tape](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/TJTG-19-use9-2.jpg "/media/lab-tapes/TJTG-19-use9-2.jpg")

Writable permanent cryogenic tape holds on vials, tubes, and boxes through LN₂ (−196°C), dry ice, and freeze–thaw. MetaliTAPE™ marks stainless-steel racks, canes, and sleeves through LN₂ and gamma. Cryo-Laminate™ is a thin overlay that protects an ID that is already on the container.

C-Kur™ tamper-evident cryo tape destroys itself if removed from vials, boxes, or cassettes. CryoSTUCK® tape sticks to already-frozen plastic, stores in LN₂ or −80°C, and tears by hand with GatorCUT™—including clear formats for marker writing.

## See-through freezer tape and a load that already ran

![TransluTAPE translucent freezer tape](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/Translucent-Freezer-Tape-FTP-24.png "/media/lab-tapes/Translucent-Freezer-Tape-FTP-24.png")

TransluTAPE™ lets you see sealed contents at −80°C and on dry ice. Removable freezer tape IDs microtubes, vials, and boxes through freeze–thaw and peels without residue.

SteriTAPE™ is writable, removable autoclave tape for reusable metal, plastic, and glass. Thermal-transfer printable autoclave tape marks trays and Genesis™ containers through steam, dry heat, gamma, and EtO. Indicator tape blackens after a steam or dry-heat cycle so you can see that the load ran.

## When the job is not a barcode

![UbiCling static cling lab tape](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/TGS-25-Use7-e1655746627896.jpg "/media/lab-tapes/TGS-25-Use7-e1655746627896.jpg")

UbiCling™ static-cling tape repositions on smooth surfaces without residue. PluroTAPE™ is perforated self-laminating tape for equipment and calibration tags. CryoHUG™ is an adhesive-free elastic wrap that seals vials, boxes, and plates after an alcohol spray.

Color Lab-TAPE™ codes cardboard, glass, plastic, and metal. Glow-in-the-dark tape marks dark rooms. Ultra-removable tape peels clean after long use. Use a bench dispenser when you are not carrying GatorCUT™.

## Tear it in a pocket

![GatorCUT™ hand-cuttable cryogenic tape](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/TGC-25-Use2-1-2.jpg "/media/gatorcut/TGC-25-Use2-1-2.jpg")

GatorCUT™ is the cutting method: cryogenic NitroTAPE™, frozen-surface CryoSTUCK® (TFNG, TXS, TXC), freezer TDF, warning TG, autoclave SteriTAPE™, FrozenSTUCK™ double-sided tape, and UbiCling™ all tear to length in a coat-pocket roll. Use it wherever scissors slow the bench down.
