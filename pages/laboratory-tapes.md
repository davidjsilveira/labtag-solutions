Tape is the ID you use when the vial, rack, or tray does not need a printed barcode.

LabTAG cryogenic, freezer, autoclave, and general-use tapes color-code samples and equipment. CryoSTUCK® tape sticks to already-frozen plastic. C-Kur™ seals tear if someone opens the box. GatorCUT™ is not a second catalog—it is the pocket roll that tears to length without scissors or a dispenser, on cryo, deep-freeze, autoclave, and static-cling tapes.

## Dewars, metal, and already-frozen plastic

![Permanent cryogenic GatorCUT tape](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/TJTG-19-use9-2.jpg "/media/lab-tapes/TJTG-19-use9-2.jpg")

Writable permanent cryogenic tape holds on vials, tubes, and boxes through LN₂ (−196°C), dry ice, and freeze–thaw. Frozen-surface tape and metal-rack tape stay on this page as tape constructs, not as the CryoSTUCK® label guide.

- Permanent cryogenic — vials, tubes, and boxes through LN₂, dry ice, and freeze–thaw
- MetaliTAPE™ — stainless-steel racks, canes, and sleeves through LN₂ and gamma
- Cryo-Laminate™ — thin overlay that protects an ID already on the container
- C-Kur™ — tamper-evident; destroys itself if removed
- CryoSTUCK® tape — already-frozen plastic; LN₂ or −80°C, including clear

## See-through freezer tape and a load that already ran

![TransluTAPE translucent freezer tape](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/Translucent-Freezer-Tape-FTP-24.png "/media/lab-tapes/Translucent-Freezer-Tape-FTP-24.png")

TransluTAPE™ lets you see sealed contents at −80°C and on dry ice. Autoclave tapes mark reusable ware and show that the load ran.

- TransluTAPE™ — see sealed contents at −80°C and on dry ice
- Removable freezer — microtubes, vials, and boxes; peels without residue
- SteriTAPE™ — writable, removable autoclave tape for reusable metal, plastic, and glass
- Printable autoclave — thermal-transfer for trays and Genesis™ containers
- Indicator tape — blackens after a steam or dry-heat cycle
