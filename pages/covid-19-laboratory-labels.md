LabTAG® provides laboratory labeling solutions for diagnostic and PCR testing kits, clinical sample workflows, cryogenic and deep-freeze storage, histological tissue processing, research and vaccine development, and sterilization of lab equipment and medical devices. Whether you need serialized kit labels, clinical trial constructs, or ready-to-print PCR, xylene, and freezer labels, samples stay identified from collection through final storage.

- **Diagnostic Kits** — Serialized and barcoded labels for sample collection and diagnostic kits

## Labels by Workflow

### Diagnostic Kits

![Cryogenic labels for diagnostic kit workflows](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/cryogenic-1.jpg "/media/covid-19-laboratory-labels/cryogenic-1.jpg")

Labels for sample collection and diagnostic kits support accurate identification from the point of collection through testing. Custom printing of serialized and/or barcoded labels is available, including in-house printing and label application in industrial quantities with on-time delivery of packaged and labeled testing kits.

### Clinical Trials

![Clinical trial laboratory labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/clinical.jpg "/media/covid-19-laboratory-labels/clinical.jpg")

Clinical trial labeling options include variable data printing, multi-part labels, full-color logos and graphics, and processes designed for accuracy of printed data—supporting research, clinical trials, and vaccine development workflows.

### Sample Processing

![PCR and sample processing laboratory labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/PCR-1.jpg "/media/covid-19-laboratory-labels/PCR-1.jpg")

Sample identification labels help ensure collected samples are safely and accurately labeled through testing. PCR tube, strip, and plate labels and tags are thermocycler-safe so samples stay securely labeled throughout the reaction. Microplate labels—including automation labels—support higher volumes with accuracy, precision, and consistency, including systems compatible with automated print-and-apply workflows.

### Histology

![Xylene-resistant laboratory labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/xylene-label.jpg "/media/covid-19-laboratory-labels/xylene-label.jpg")

Xylene-resistant labels support histological tissue processing where chemical exposure would compromise ordinary print and adhesive—keeping specimen IDs legible through staining and related protocols.

### Cold Storage

![Deep-freeze and cryogenic laboratory labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/deep-freeze-1.jpg "/media/covid-19-laboratory-labels/deep-freeze-1.jpg")

Deep-freeze and cryogenic labels support freezing, biobanking, and tracking of patient samples in ultra-low and liquid nitrogen storage conditions so IDs remain affixed and scannable through long-term cold-chain storage.

### Sterilization

![Autoclave sterilization laboratory labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/autoclave-1.jpg "/media/covid-19-laboratory-labels/autoclave-1.jpg")

Labels suited to autoclave and related sterilization protocols identify lab equipment and medical devices through steam sterilization cycles without losing adhesive performance or print legibility.
