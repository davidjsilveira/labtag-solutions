## Sample Processing and Ready-to-Print Labels

### PCR, Plates, and Automation

In addition to kit labeling, we offer labels for sample collection and testing. Sample identification labels help ensure collected samples are safely and accurately labeled. For sample processing, PCR tube, strip, and plate labels and tags are thermocycler-safe so samples stay securely labeled throughout the reaction. Microplate labels—including automation labels—support higher volumes with accuracy, precision, and consistency. Labels compatible with automated print-and-apply systems help handle increased sample throughput.

### Xylene-Resistant Labels

![Xylene-resistant laboratory labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/xylene-label.jpg "/media/covid-19-laboratory-labels/xylene-label.jpg")

Xylene-resistant labels support histological tissue processing where chemical exposure would compromise ordinary print and adhesive.

### Deep-Freeze and Cryogenic Labels

![Deep-freeze laboratory labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/deep-freeze-1.jpg "/media/covid-19-laboratory-labels/deep-freeze-1.jpg")

Deep-freeze and cryogenic labels support freezing, biobanking, and tracking of patient samples in ultra-low and liquid nitrogen storage conditions.
