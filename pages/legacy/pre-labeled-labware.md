# Pre-Labeled Labware

Source: [https://www.labtag.com/solutions/pre-labeled-labware/](https://www.labtag.com/solutions/pre-labeled-labware/)

![Pre-labeled labware kitting service](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Kitting-Service-7v2.jpg "/media/pre-labeled-labware/Kitting-Service-7v2.jpg")

Purchasing and labeling labware in high volumes can be a burden for busy laboratory teams. LabTAG’s pre-labeled labware service lets our experts print and label your tubes, vials, and other labware according to your specifications. You can send us your labware or let us procure it for a hands-off, stress-free workflow.

Personalized labeling covers custom barcodes, variable text, graphics and logos, precise application, and barcode grading for quality assurance and accuracy before shipment to your lab.

### By Service Model

- **Send Your Labware** — Ship your tubes, vials, or other labware; we label to your specifications
- **We Procure** — We source the labware you need and ship it fully labeled to your lab

### By Capability

- **Custom Barcodes & Text** — 1D/2D barcodes and variable text matched to your protocol
- **Graphics & Logos** — Printed branding applied with the labels
- **Precise Application** — Labels applied to labware according to your placement requirements
- **Barcode Grading** — Quality checks to ensure scannable, accurate barcodes
- **Material Range** — Wide selection of label materials suited to your storage and handling conditions

## Personalized to Your Exact Needs

### Custom Barcodes & Text

![Custom barcodes and variable text on labware](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Kitting-Service-7v2.jpg "/media/pre-labeled-labware/Kitting-Service-7v2.jpg")

Print custom 1D and 2D barcodes plus variable text matched to your protocol, study design, or LIMS requirements—so every tube and vial arrives ready for scanning and tracking.

### Graphics & Logos

![Labware labeled with graphics and logos](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/Kitting-Service-1v2.jpg "/media/pre-labeled-labware/Kitting-Service-1v2.jpg")

Add printed graphics and logos alongside your identifiers for branding, color coding, or study-specific marks applied with the labels—not as a separate step in your lab.

### Precise Application

![Precisely applied labels on tubes and vials](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Kitting-Service-7v2.jpg "/media/pre-labeled-labware/Kitting-Service-7v2.jpg")

Labels are applied to labware according to your placement requirements—cap, side, wrap, or other positions—so orientation and coverage stay consistent across large batches.

### Barcode Grading

![Barcode grading for scannable labware IDs](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/Kitting-Service-1v2.jpg "/media/pre-labeled-labware/Kitting-Service-1v2.jpg")

Barcode grading checks ensure print quality and accuracy before shipment, reducing failed scans and rework when samples enter your workflow.

### Material Range

![Label materials for diverse labware conditions](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Kitting-Service-7v2.jpg "/media/pre-labeled-labware/Kitting-Service-7v2.jpg")

Choose from a wide range of label materials suited to your storage and handling conditions—including cryogenic, deep-freeze, chemical-resistant, and general laboratory constructs.
