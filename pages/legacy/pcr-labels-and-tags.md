# PCR Labels and Tags

Source: [https://www.labtag.com/solutions/pcr-labels-and-tags/](https://www.labtag.com/solutions/pcr-labels-and-tags/)

![PCR Labels and Tags](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/FJT-557-use13.jpg "/media/pcr-labels-and-tags/FJT-557-use13.jpg")

LabTAG® provides labels, tags, and label dots for 0.2 mL PCR tubes, high-profile 8-well PCR strips, and skirted, semi-skirted, and non-skirted PCR plates. Across academic and pharma labs, these durable identifiers keep PCR samples tracked through sample prep, thermal cycling, analysis, and cold storage.

Suitable for clinical PCR, qPCR/RT-PCR, molecular biology research, forensics, and mRNA workflows, with laser and thermal-transfer printing plus color coding, barcode, and hand-writable options for clearer sample tracking.

### By Vessel

- **PCR Tubes** — Dot and rectangular labels for 0.2 mL tubes and caps
- **PCR Strips** — Perforated PCR-TAG™ labels for high-profile 8-well strips
- **IDenti-PCR™** — Column labels for breakable 96-well PCR plates
- **PlateTAG™** — Side labels for skirted and semi-skirted 96- and 384-well plates
- **PCR-TagTrax®** — Adhesive-free tags for strips and semi-skirted plates
- **Applicators** — Tools that speed PCR-TagTrax® and small-dot application

### By Feature

- **Cryogenic Adhesive** — Holds through thermal cycling and deep-freeze storage
- **Color Coding** — Multiple colors for visual sample tracking across runs
- **Barcode Ready** — Laser and thermal-transfer printers for 1D/2D barcodes
- **Hand Writable** — Compatible with cryogenic markers for manual labeling
- **Removable** — PlateTAG™ peels clean without leaving adhesive residue

## PCR Labels by Vessel

### PCR Tubes

![Cryogenic PCR tube and cap labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/FJT-269-Use-8.jpg "/media/pcr-labels-and-tags/FJT-269-Use-8.jpg")

Dot and rectangular labels designed to be applied to single PCR tubes. Easy application and a strong permanent thermal/cryo adhesive ensure proper sample identification during multi-hour PCR runs. Compatible with laser and thermal-transfer printers, as well as cryogenic markers for hand labeling. Multiple color choices and barcode capability further enhance accurate sample tracking.

### PCR Strips

![PCR-TAG strip labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/FJT-269-Use-5.jpg "/media/pcr-labels-and-tags/FJT-269-Use-5.jpg")

PCR-TAG™ labels are designed for high-profile 8-well PCR tube strips. Apply a single label to an entire strip, break the perforations, and finish applying as a single label to each tube. This eliminates tedious removal from backing and application of individual labels. Compatible with thermal-transfer printers and cryogenic markers for hand labeling, with multiple color choices for accurate sample tracking.

### IDenti-PCR™

![IDenti-PCR plate labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/PID-1-use.png "/media/pcr-labels-and-tags/PID-1-use.png")

IDenti-PCR™ labels adhere to the top lip area of the long side of a 96-well PCR plate for independent column identification. Label perforations also allow breakable PCR plates to be separated into 8-well strips while leaving individual labels intact. This solution provides an economical alternative to custom barcoded (embossed) microplates.

### PlateTAG™

![PlateTAG PCR plate labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/AMA-11-use.jpg "/media/pcr-labels-and-tags/AMA-11-use.jpg")

PlateTAG™ labels adhere easily to the long or short side of 96- and 384-well skirted and semi-skirted PCR plates. Designed for thermal cycler and long-term deep-freeze storage (−80°C) use. Remains intact through an entire PCR workflow while still removable without leaving adhesive residue. Barcoded and RFID labels are available to increase sample traceability.

### PCR-TagTrax®

![PCR-TagTrax non-adhesive tags](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/Tags.png "/media/pcr-labels-and-tags/Tags.png")

PCR-TagTrax® adhesive-free tags support temporary or long-term sample tracking through every stage of a PCR workflow, from setup through thermal cycling to cold storage. Easy application to 8-tube strips or non-skirted and semi-skirted 96-well PCR plates. Label perforation allows linked tracking on tubes and in laboratory notebooks or batch records. Color coding and barcode addition increase sample tracking ability.

### Applicators

![PCR label applicators](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/PTA-1-Tag-Aid-Use22-1.jpg "/media/pcr-labels-and-tags/PTA-1-Tag-Aid-Use22-1.jpg")

PCR-TagTrax® applicators and PikaTAG® label applicator kits help place adhesive-free tags and small-diameter dots on tubes and strips. Patented applicator designs support consistent application when labeling PCR vessels by hand.

## PCR Labels by Feature

### Cryogenic Adhesive

![Cryogenic adhesive PCR tube labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/PCRJTT-269-use2.jpg "/media/pcr-labels-and-tags/PCRJTT-269-use2.jpg")

PCR labels use a strong permanent thermal/cryo adhesive that stays bonded through multi-hour thermal cycling, freeze–thaw cycles, and deep-freeze storage (−80°C). That bond keeps tube, strip, and plate IDs in place from sample prep through analysis and cold storage.

### Color Coding

![Color-coded PCR labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/FJT-269-Use-2.jpg "/media/pcr-labels-and-tags/FJT-269-Use-2.jpg")

Multiple colors support visual sample tracking across tubes, strips, and plates so runs, conditions, and cohorts stay distinguishable at a glance—alongside barcodes when you need full digital traceability.

### Barcode Ready

![Thermal-transfer barcode printable PCR labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/Thermal-Transfer-Roll-Format-Buttonsv3.jpg "/media/pcr-labels-and-tags/Thermal-Transfer-Roll-Format-Buttonsv3.jpg")

Compatible with laser and thermal-transfer printers for crisp 1D and 2D barcodes. Print patient, sample, or assay IDs on demand so PCR vessels scan cleanly through automated and manual workflows.

### Hand Writable

![Hand-writable PCR labels and markers](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/AdobeStock_304078731.jpeg "/media/pcr-labels-and-tags/AdobeStock_304078731.jpeg")

Accepts smudge-proof cryogenic markers for manual labeling at the bench when a printer is not at hand. Hand-writable PCR dots and rectangles are ideal for quick notes, temporary IDs, and hybrid print-plus-write workflows.

### Removable

![Removable PlateTAG PCR plate labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/AMA-11-use.jpg "/media/pcr-labels-and-tags/AMA-11-use.jpg")

Clean peel-away options—including PlateTAG™ side labels—remove without leaving adhesive residue when temporary PCR identification is enough. Remains intact through thermal cycling and deep-freeze storage, then lifts cleanly when the vessel is reused or re-labeled.
