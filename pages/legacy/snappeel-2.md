# SnapPEEL™

Effortless labeling of tube tops & sides

SnapPEEL™ technology makes it easy for lab workers to identify cryovials and microtubes with its 2-in-1 rectangle + circle combination.

## The Technology

### What is SnapPEEL™?

![](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/Michael_Animation_02_Aria_V06.mp4)

SnapPEEL is a combination of a circular label and rectangular label that can be peeled off the liner together and snapped apart during application.

## The Process

### Peel once, apply twice

Peeling tiny circle labels can be frustrating. But labeling the top of a vial is crucial. SnapPEEL™ releaves the pain and maintains the gains of labeling tube tops

### 1. Peel

![](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/SnapPEEL-Step1.jpg)

Peel the label off of the liner

### 2. Apply

![](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/SnapPEEL-Step2.jpg)

Apply the circle label

### 3. Snap

![](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/SnapPEEL-Step3.jpg)

Snap the labels apart

### 4. Apply

![](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/SnapPEEL-Step4.jpg)

Apply the rectangle label

## The Benefits

### Work smarter, not harder

### Precision

The rectangle label acts as handle for applying the circle labels accurately

### Efficiency

Saving time and effort by streamlining the labeling process
