A lab print station is printer, media, ribbon, and software that talk to the same LIMS.

LabTAG specs kits and benchtop printers—including the Cody-3™—so sample IDs print the same way every day, with scientists on technical support. If you would rather not own a printer, LabTAG prints roll, sheet, and fanfold for you.

## Start with a kit, not leftover media

![Zebra printing kit](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/Zebra-Printing-Kit.png "/media/label-printing-solutions/Zebra-Printing-Kit.png")

Kits ship printer, matched labels, ribbon, and software together. The first roll is in spec for the workflow instead of a generic office driver plus whatever media is in the drawer. Ribbons are chosen for the label face and the chemicals it will see. Handheld laser scanners and 2D imagers connect over USB or RS-232. Cables, ribbon cores, applicators, and dispensers round out the station.

## The benchtop we put on the spec

![GA International Cody-3™ thermal printer](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Cody-3-use3-1.jpg "/media/ga-international-cody-3-printer/Cody-3-use3-1.jpg")

Cody-3™ is the LabTAG benchtop: 300 dpi direct-thermal or thermal-transfer, rolls from 1″ to 4.64″ wide, USB, serial, and ethernet. BarTender software, cables, and ribbon hardware ship in the box. Switch modes to print cryogenic, chemical-resistant, and general LabTAG stocks without a second printer.

Desktop and industrial printers from other brands are available when throughput or width needs more than a bench unit.

## Design templates versus storing samples

![Label design and barcode printing software](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/printing-labels.png "/media/label-software/printing-labels.png")

Use design software (including BarTender®) to build templates—text, barcodes, logos, and a database merge—for roll, sheet, and fanfold. That software does not store samples. LIMS, LIS, and ELN systems do.

Some informatics tools include a design module; others connect to LabTAG labels. Partners include LabMate, LDMS, Fluics CONNECT, eIVF, Labguru, GenoFAB, SciSure, Genemod, Clustermarket, QRlogix, ATMPS, and CYBRA.
