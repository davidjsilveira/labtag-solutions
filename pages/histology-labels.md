## Labels for Microscope Slides

### Xylene-Resistant Slide Labels

![Xylene-resistant microscope slide labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/XAL-67SB-use1.jpg "/media/histology-labels/XAL-67SB-use1.jpg")

Excellent stain resistance makes these labels ideal for glass microscope slides used in histology. Recommended for routine as well as special staining protocols, they resist xylene, toluene, hexanes, alcohols, acetone, eosin Y, hematoxylin, and safer non-toxic xylene alternatives. Suitable for use with automated slide processors and compatible with all common thermal-transfer barcode printers.

### Histo-PinTAG™

![Histo-PinTAG slide re-identification](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/Guide-Microscope-Slide-Label-Sizing-Guide-Cover.jpg "/media/histology-labels/Guide-Microscope-Slide-Label-Sizing-Guide-Cover.jpg")

Unique design for quick and easy microscope slide re-labeling and covering up existing 2D barcodes. Removable adhesive ensures the label can be removed without damaging the label underneath or leaving adhesive residue. Vibrant yellow color indicates that the microscope slides have been re-identified. Printable with most commercially available thermal-transfer printers.

### HistoLAM™

Self-laminating labels for microscope slides with an over-laminating flap that protects the print from long-term immersion in xylene. Perfect for identifying slides throughout slide processing and various staining procedures, including antigen retrieval in immunohistochemistry protocols. Compatible with existing label printing systems and tissue microarray systems such as the Galileo TMA CK4600 system.

## Labels for Paraffin Wax Blocks

### ParafiTAG™

![ParafiTAG paraffin block labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/AFT-1-use2.jpg "/media/histology-labels/AFT-1-use2.jpg")

Adheres to hard-to-stick surfaces, including paraffin wax used for histology applications. Withstands a range of temperatures (-55°C/-67°F to +70°C/+158°F) and is suitable for use on recently cooled wax blocks. Resistant to harsh chemicals and solvents, including xylene, toluene, hexanes, alcohols, acetone, and methyl ethyl ketone (MEK). Compatible with all common thermal-transfer barcode printers.

### ParafiGARD™

Metal-detectable labels for paraffin wax blocks. A metallic layer allows labeled paraffin wax blocks to be tracked using a metal detector, improving sample tracking and helping prevent accidental loss of paraffin blocks and histology samples. Adheres to hard-to-stick paraffin surfaces and resists a wide range of harsh chemicals and solvents, including xylene and alcohols.

### RFID Paraffin Block Labels

Chemical-resistant RFID labels for paraffin wax blocks incorporate an encodable inlay for improved cassette management and enhanced tracking. Designed to integrate with specimen management systems, they can also carry printed 1D/2D barcodes for added traceability in LIMS or hospital workflows.
