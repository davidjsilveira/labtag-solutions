# Custom Laboratory Labels

LabTAG® has the know-how, flexibility, tools, and material selection to provide custom laboratory labels. With years of experience in the life sciences industry and label manufacturing, our experts create tailored solutions that combine die cutting, full-color graphics, and variable data printing (VDP). Ask about DataGARD™—laminated VDP labels that protect serialized data.

Choose the right shape, size, material, and configuration from our inventory—or design a new ISO 9001:2015 certified solution when a standard product is not available.

### By Capability

- **Die Cut** — Custom shape, size, and material from stock or made-to-order
- **Variable Data Printing** — Serialized data and barcodes from a database
- **Full-Color Graphics** — Logos via flexographic or short-run digital print
- **Barcode Grading** — Verified 1D and 2D barcodes with TruCheck Omni™
- **DataGARD™** — Laminated VDP for protected, scannable serialization

### By Specification

- **Formats** — Roll, sheet, die cut, fanfold, butt cut, and multi-part
- **Printing** — Laser, flexographic, thermal, and inkjet on rolls or sheets
- **Materials** — Foil, film, paper, matte, glossy, and laminated
- **Resistance** — Waterproof, weatherproof, heat-, and chemical-resistant
- **Surfaces** — Vials, tubes, syringes, slides, paraffin blocks, and more
- **Data Formats** — Databases, spreadsheets, barcodes, and vector artwork

## Custom Label Capabilities

### Die Cut

![Custom die-cut laboratory labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/Die-Cut-Labels.jpg "/media/custom-laboratory-labels/Die-Cut-Labels.jpg")

We help you select the right shape, size, material, and configuration from our inventory of 6000+ products. When an existing labeling solution is not available, our in-house team of scientists will design and create one. Working directly with a manufacturer means durable materials and rigorous quality testing.

### Variable Data Printing

![Variable data printing on custom lab labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/Variable-Data-Printing-Services-2.jpg "/media/custom-laboratory-labels/Variable-Data-Printing-Services-2.jpg")

Our custom laboratory label printing service accurately prints variable, serialized data from a database or spreadsheet as well as 1D and 2D barcodes. This hands-off approach helps operations run more efficiently—supply your data and we handle the printing.

### Full-Color Graphics

![Full-color custom laboratory labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/Full-Color-Graphics_.jpg "/media/custom-laboratory-labels/Full-Color-Graphics_.jpg")

Bring custom labels to life with full-color graphics. Add your logo to asset identification tags or branded products. In-house experts can design a custom concept for your label. We support high-volume flexographic printing and short-run digital printing with short lead times.

### Barcode Grading

![Barcode grading for custom laboratory labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/Barcode-Grading.jpg "/media/custom-laboratory-labels/Barcode-Grading.jpg")

We provide graded 1D and 2D barcodes to guarantee accurate and consistent scanning. Using the TruCheck Omni™ grading system, we verify and grade 1D linear barcodes as well as 2D symbols. Barcoding services support biomedical research sample tracking and verified barcodes for therapeutic drug containers, medical devices, and cosmetic products.

### DataGARD™

![Laminated variable data printing with DataGARD](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/Variable-Data-Printing-Services-2.jpg "/media/custom-laboratory-labels/Variable-Data-Printing-Services-2.jpg")

DataGARD™ laminated VDP keeps printouts legible and scannable through critical applications. Serialized data and barcodes stay protected under a durable laminate—ideal when variable IDs must survive handling, chemicals, or abrasion after printing.

## Custom Label Specifications

### Formats

![Custom label formats and die cuts](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/Die-Cut-Labels.jpg "/media/custom-laboratory-labels/Die-Cut-Labels.jpg")

Available formats include roll, sheet, die cut, fanfold, piggyback, butt cut, continuous, front & back, and multi-part labels—matched to your printers, applicators, and packaging workflow.

### Printing

![Custom label printing methods](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/Full-Color-Graphics_.jpg "/media/custom-laboratory-labels/Full-Color-Graphics_.jpg")

Printing methods include laser (digital), direct thermal, thermal-transfer, flexographic, and inkjet on rolls or sheets—so you can choose the process that fits volume, durability, and graphic needs.

### Materials

![Custom label material selection](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/Die-Cut-Labels.jpg "/media/custom-laboratory-labels/Die-Cut-Labels.jpg")

Material options include foil, film, paper, matte, glossy, holographic, laminated, and varnished constructs for laboratory and industrial identification.

### Resistance

![Durable resistant custom laboratory labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/Barcode-Grading.jpg "/media/custom-laboratory-labels/Barcode-Grading.jpg")

Resistance options cover cryogenic, waterproof, weatherproof, heat-resistant, scratch-resistant, and chemical-resistant performance for harsh lab and field environments.

### Surfaces

![Custom labels for vials tubes and labware](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/Variable-Data-Printing-Services-2.jpg "/media/custom-laboratory-labels/Variable-Data-Printing-Services-2.jpg")

Application surfaces include vials and tubes, glass tubes, syringes, blood bags, metal racks, resin embedding, microscope slides, paraffin wax blocks, vitrification devices, and plastic containers or boxes.

### Data Formats

![Custom label data and barcode formats](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/Barcode-Grading.jpg "/media/custom-laboratory-labels/Barcode-Grading.jpg")

Supply data via database, barcoding software, 1D/2D/QR barcodes, vector formats (AI, EPS), and spreadsheets (CSV, Excel)—we print from the formats your systems already use.
