# RFID Labels & RFID Tags

Source: [https://www.labtag.com/solutions/rfid-labels-tags/](https://www.labtag.com/solutions/rfid-labels-tags/)

![RFID NitroTAG cryogenic labels with printer and reader](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/RF3-JTTA-9-use2-1.jpg "/media/rfid-labels-tags/RF3-JTTA-9-use2-1.jpg")

Radio-frequency identification (RFID) helps hospitals, histopathology labs, research facilities, and IVF labs with record keeping, access control, inventory tracking, and sample management. Our RFID solutions use flexible NFC, HF, and UHF inlays on durable resistant labels, creating RFID labels suited to harsh laboratory and clinical environments for sample tracking.

RFID smart labels support batch reading without line of sight and print serialized text and barcodes on compatible thermal-transfer printers alongside encoded RFID data.

## RFID Labels by Resistance

### Cryogenic RFID Vial Labels

Cryogenic RFID vial labels support record keeping, inventory tracking, and sample management in hospitals, research facilities, and IVF labs. Flexible passive inlays allow the labels to be applied to curved surfaces, and thermal-transfer printing supports serialized text and barcodes alongside encoded RFID data.

### Autoclave-Resistant RFID Labels

Autoclave-resistant RFID labels create RFID identification suited to harsh sterilization environments, supporting inventory tracking and sample management through sterilization workflows.

### Deep-Freeze RFID Microplate Labels

![RFID-PlateTAG labels on microplates with handheld reader](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/RFP-550-use3.jpg "/media/rfid-labels-tags/RFP-550-use3.jpg")

Deep-freeze RFID microplate labels support batch scanning without requiring direct line of sight. RFID smart labels can be scanned simultaneously from a few inches to a few feet in range, even when covered or embedded within a container, improving inventory accuracy and workflow monitoring in the lab.

### Xylene & Chemical-Resistant RFID Labels

![RFID XyliTUFF labels for microscope slides](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/RF3-FTT-98-use5.jpg "/media/rfid-labels-tags/RF3-FTT-98-use5.jpg")

Xylene and chemical-resistant RFID labels are suited to histopathology labs for record keeping and sample tracking. Customization options can utilize durable label facestocks to produce an RFID label that can withstand chemical exposure and a wide range of temperatures.

## RFID Hardware

### RFID Printers

![Zebra RFID printer for thermal-transfer RFID labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/ZD621R-800x600px.jpg "/media/rfid-labels-tags/ZD621R-800x600px.jpg")

Thermal RFID labels are compatible with most thermal-transfer printers, allowing them to be printed with serialized text and barcodes. This combination of barcodes and RFID makes it possible to fully integrate RFID tags and labels into a laboratory management system, speeding up routine processes such as sample gatekeeping, inventorying, and tracking.

### RFID Readers

Our RFID smart labels are equipped with a passive inlay that does not require a battery to function. Passive RFID labels and RFID tags do, however, require an integrator system that will store and process the encoded information. Handheld readers support omnidirectional scanning without full visibility or direct line of sight.

## Custom RFID Identification Solutions

![PingTAG flexible RFID tags with handheld reader](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/RF3-VPT-73BM-use3-2.jpg "/media/rfid-labels-tags/RF3-VPT-73BM-use3-2.jpg")

In addition to our printable RFID labels and RFID tags, RFID solutions can be custom printed with serialized barcodes, color logos, and encoded with alphanumerical values. We also offer TID and UID reading and printing on RFID labels and flexible/non-adhesive RFID tags. This service includes a verification system to verify the unique RFID code matches the printed data. TID and UID are factory-locked and cannot be changed following production. This simplifies the process of detecting if an RFID tag has been replaced, perfect for instances where tamper detection is crucial, such as anti-counterfeiting.

Our full customization options can utilize our durable label facestocks to produce an RFID label that can withstand chemical exposure and a wide range of temperatures. From inlay, antennae, chip, and size to configuration and material selection, GA International offers performant specialty RFID labels and RFID tags to produce unique and dependable identification and tracking solutions.
