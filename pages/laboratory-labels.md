## Laboratory Label Guide

Everything you need to know before choosing the right laboratory labels. Browse options by application or resistance below.

### By Application

- **Cryogenic** — Labels for liquid nitrogen and ultra-low temperature storage across research, clinical, fertility, and biobank workflows
- **PCR** — Labels and tags for PCR tubes, strips, and plates through thermal cycling and cold storage
- **IVF** — Identification solutions for Assisted Reproductive Technology clinics and labs
- **Clinical Trials** — Custom clinical labeling to meet sponsor, patient, and regulatory demands
- **COVID-19** — Labels supporting pharmaceutical and healthcare COVID-19 testing and sample tracking
- **Cannabis** — Labels for growing, packaging, testing, and seed-to-sale traceability

### By Resistance

- **Xylene & Chemical** — Laminate-free chemical-resistant labels for histology, pathology, and chemistry labs
- **Autoclave** — High-temperature sterilization labels for steam, dry heat, gamma, and EtO workflows
- **Deep-Freeze** — Freezer-grade labels for −80°C/−20°C storage and dry-ice transport

## Product Families

### Cryogenic Labels

![Cryogenic laboratory labels on vials](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/JTTA-103-Use-vials.png "/media/laboratory-labels/JTTA-103-Use-vials.png")

LabTAG® provides cryogenic labels designed to withstand extended storage in liquid or vapor phase liquid nitrogen and ultra-low temperature freezers. Available in a range of sizes and configurations, and compatible with thermal and digital printers, with permanent or removable adhesive and features such as blackout, tamper-evident, and wrap-around options.

### Xylene-Resistant Labels

![Xylene-resistant laboratory labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/xylene-label.jpg "/media/laboratory-labels/xylene-label.jpg")

LabTAG® provides xylene-resistant labels, ribbons, and tags for clinical and medical research labs, including histology, pathology, and chemistry labs. These laminate-free chemical-resistant labels withstand direct exposure to and immersion in xylene, toluene, alcohols, formalin, and other harsh solvents.

### Autoclave Labels

![Autoclave-resistant labels on glass bottles in sterilizer](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/autoclave-1.jpg "/media/laboratory-labels/autoclave-1.jpg")

LabTAG provides high-temperature autoclave-resistant labels for materials that require sterilization. These durable labels have been tested for use in gamma irradiators, steam autoclaves, dry heat ovens, and ethylene oxide gas chambers, with options for thermal-transfer, laser, and inkjet printers.

### Deep-Freeze Labels

![Deep-freeze laboratory labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/deep-freeze-1.jpg "/media/laboratory-labels/deep-freeze-1.jpg")

LabTAG® offers deep-freeze labels that withstand short and long-term storage in ultra-low temperature laboratory freezers (−80°C/−112°F, −20°C/−4°F) and transportation on dry ice. Suited for microtubes, vials, freezer boxes, blood bags, microplates, and PCR tubes and strips.

### PCR Labels and Tags

![PCR labels and tags](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/PCR-1.jpg "/media/laboratory-labels/PCR-1.jpg")

LabTAG provides labels, tags, and label dots for 0.2 mL PCR tubes, high-profile 8-well PCR strips, and skirted, semi-skirted, and non-skirted PCR plates so samples stay identified through setup, thermal cycling, and cold storage.
