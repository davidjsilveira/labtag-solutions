A histology ID has to come through xylene, stain, and the scanner still scannable. The same chemical-resistant family covers the slide, the paraffin block, the bottle of acid on the bench, and the slide box in the −80°C freezer.

When a consulting lab receives a slide that already has someone else’s barcode, over-labeling has to add an internal ID without stacking thickness that jams a coverslipper. That is the same article as the xylene-resistant slide label—not a second histology page.

## Glass that has to survive the processor

![Xylene-resistant microscope slide labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/XAL-67SB-use1.jpg "/media/histology-labels/XAL-67SB-use1.jpg")

Thermal-transfer slide labels resist xylene, toluene, hexanes, alcohols, acetone, eosin Y, hematoxylin, and safer xylene substitutes. Laminate-free faces do not pick up stain color. They run on automated slide processors and the barcode printers already in the lab.

HistoLAM™ folds a clear flap over the print when IHC antigen retrieval or a long xylene bath would otherwise erase the ID.

## Wax that does not want adhesive

![ParafiTAG paraffin block labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/ParafiTAG-labels-for-tissue-embedding-in-paraffin-blocks-3-e1564072755821.png "/media/histology-labels/ParafiTAG-paraffin-blocks.png")

ParafiTAG™ bonds to recently cooled paraffin and FFPE cassettes from −55°C to +70°C, and holds through xylene, alcohols, acetone, and MEK. Print barcodes for tissue microarrays, including Galileo TMA CK4600 workflows.

ParafiGARD™ adds a metallic layer so a block that leaves the rack can be found with a metal detector without giving up xylene resistance. Chemical-resistant RFID paraffin labels encode cassette IDs for specimen systems and can still carry a printed 1D/2D barcode.

## Incoming slides that already have an ID

![Histo-PinTAG microscope slide over-labeling](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/AZA-559-Step3-1-scaled-1-1.jpg "/media/microscope-slide-over-labeling/AZA-559-Step3-1-scaled-1-1.jpg")

Histo-PinTAG™ covers the old 2D code with a dot and snaps off a rectangle for the new internal ID below the original label. Removable adhesive peels clean; yellow flags the re-ID. Original case data stays visible. Coverslip thickness stays in spec.

Full blackout cover-ups replace an entire label when nothing original should show. Clear chemical-resistant overlays protect or add data without hiding print. DYMO-compatible reprints handle alcohols, xylene, and cryo storage at the bench when the volume is a handful of slides, not a processor.

## Bottles next to the stainer

![Chemical-resistant bottle and labware labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/FTT-152-Use1-605x348px.jpg "/media/xylene-chemical-resistant-labels/FTT-152-Use1-605x348px.jpg")

Permanent or removable solvent-resistant labels color-code bottles, beakers, cryo tubes, vials, and slide boxes. Adhesive conforms to small diameters, survives LN₂ and freeze–thaw, and withstands ethanol, isopropanol, and formalin. Freezer-grade box labels store to −80°C.

Permanent faces for concentrated sulfuric acid, TCA, and sodium hydroxide keep reagent containers identified through immersion—not only splash. Pair the labels with xylene-resistant ribbons, printers, and scanners as a histology print kit.
