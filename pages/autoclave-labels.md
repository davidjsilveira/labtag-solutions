# Autoclave Labels

Source: [https://www.labtag.com/solutions/autoclave-labels/](https://www.labtag.com/solutions/autoclave-labels/)

![Autoclave Labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/AUTT-94-use-2.png "/media/autoclave-labels/AUTT-94-use-2.png")

LabTAG provides high-temperature autoclave-resistant labels for the identification of materials that require sterilization. These durable labels are tested for gamma irradiation, steam autoclaves, dry heat, and ethylene oxide. A high-performance adhesive bonds to metal, glass, and plastic containers, as well as Genesis™ sterilization containers used across laboratory workflows.

We offer sterilization labels for thermal-transfer, laser, and inkjet printers, with a range of additional options including wrap-around, blackout, indicator strip, and piggyback formats.

### By Printing Method

- **Thermal-Transfer** — Steri-ThermoTAG™ labels for barcode printers and serialized data
- **Laser** — Steri-LazrTAG™ sheet labels for desktop and office laser printers
- **Inkjet** — Steri-JetTAG™ full-color sheet labels for common inkjet printers

### By Feature

- **Wrap-Around** — Self-laminating protection for small-diameter containers
- **Piggyback** — Multi-layer labels for tracking and documentation
- **Blackout** — Cover-up labels that permanently conceal prior print
- **RFID** — Encoded labels for sterilization traceability systems

### By Adhesive

- **Permanent** — Bonds through repeated sterilization cycles
- **Removable** — Clean peel-away when temporary ID is enough

## Autoclave Labels by Printing Method

### Thermal-Transfer

![Thermal-Transfer autoclave labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/AUTT-94-use-2.png "/media/autoclave-labels/AUTT-94-use-2.png")
![Thermal-Transfer autoclave tape](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/AUTR-56-use-2.png "/media/autoclave-labels/AUTR-56-use-2.png")

Steri-ThermoTAG™ autoclave-resistant thermal-transfer labels are made from heat-resistant thermoplastic material and can withstand temperatures ranging from -60°C to +150°C as well as exposure to alcohols and detergents. Perfect for labeling materials with high-quality barcodes and serialized information.

These labels are provided with a permanent or removable adhesive with special features like a self-laminating flap, transparent or opaque material, and a practical sterilization indicator strip. We also provide thermal-transfer printable autoclave-resistant tape.

### Laser

![Laser autoclave labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/ALTR-13-use2.png "/media/autoclave-labels/ALTR-13-use2.png")
![Laser autoclave label sheets](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/ALTR-13-use.png "/media/autoclave-labels/ALTR-13-use.png")

Steri-LazrTAG™ are waterproof and durable autoclavable laser labels that can be conveniently printed using desktop or office laser printers. A versatile option, these sterilization labels are available in letter and A4 formats, can be permanent or removable, and come with additional features like transparency, self-lamination, and wrap-around formatting. Ideal for identifying surgical instruments, stainless steel and aluminum surfaces, trays, glassware, double-blister packs, and more.

### Inkjet

![Inkjet autoclave label assortment](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/INKA-Labels-Mix3.png "/media/autoclave-labels/INKA-Labels-Mix3.png")
![Inkjet autoclave labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/INKA-Labels-Mix.png "/media/autoclave-labels/INKA-Labels-Mix.png")

Steri-JetTAG™ are heat-resistant inkjet labels that come with a strong permanent adhesive that withstands spraying with ethanol, isopropanol, and other alcohols, and can resist continuous wiping and rubbing with surface sanitizing wipes.

Our autoclave inkjet printable labels are durable, weatherproof, waterproof, and have been tested to endure multiple autoclave cycles. Compatible with all common desktop inkjet printer brands, they can be printed with full-color text, graphics, and logos. We provide free Microsoft Word online templates for quick and easy formatting.

## Autoclave Labels by Feature

### Wrap-Around

![Wrap-around autoclave labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/DFSL-13-Use-Autoclave-1.png "/media/autoclave-labels/DFSL-13-Use-Autoclave-1.png")

Wrap-around laser and thermal-transfer autoclave labels are ideal for identifying small-diameter round containers as well as materials destined for sterilization or storage in ultra-low temperature conditions. These high-temperature and cryogenic-resistant labels can withstand a wide range of temperatures, from -196°C/-321°F up to +150°C/+302°F. These versatile self-laminating labels are protected by a transparent laminate that wraps around and covers the label, providing additional protection against abrasion, chemical exposure, and scratching.

### Piggyback

![Piggyback autoclave labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/PAUTR-6-use2.png "/media/autoclave-labels/PAUTR-6-use2.png")
![Piggyback autoclave label layers](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/PAUTR-2-use-2.png "/media/autoclave-labels/PAUTR-2-use-2.png")

Thermal-transfer printable, removable, autoclave-resistant piggyback labels are resistant to high temperatures and have a multi-layer design, providing a versatile identification solution for various containers and surfaces. Their piggyback design and ability to be printed with 1D and 2D barcodes make them the ideal choice for tracking materials, keeping document trails intact, and improving your overall level of organization. The smaller piggyback labels can be used to keep track of what material has been sterilized in your lab book or on additional paperwork.

### Blackout

![Blackout autoclave labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/BOA-19-use2-1.png "/media/autoclave-labels/BOA-19-use2-1.png")
![Blackout autoclave cover-up labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/BOA-19-use3-1.png "/media/autoclave-labels/BOA-19-use3-1.png")

The autoclavable cover-up labels permanently conceal previously printed information, including pre-existing labels printed with text and/or barcodes. Blackout Steri-ThermoTAG™ labels have a strong permanent adhesive that ensures the labels adhere strongly to both flat and curved surfaces and won’t wrinkle or peel even at elevated temperatures.

Certified by Underwriters Laboratories (UL) and Conformité Européene (CE), these block-out sterilization labels are ideal for containers marked with sensitive or confidential information that need to be autoclaved before disposal, including biohazard and radioactive material.

### RFID

![RFID autoclave labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/RF3-AUTT-28-use5.jpg "/media/autoclave-labels/RF3-AUTT-28-use5.jpg")
![RFID autoclave label application](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/RF3-AUTT-28-use1.jpg "/media/autoclave-labels/RF3-AUTT-28-use1.jpg")
![RFID autoclave sterilization labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/RF3-AUTT-28-use3.jpg "/media/autoclave-labels/RF3-AUTT-28-use3.jpg")

Autoclave-resistant RFID labels have a strong permanent adhesive and can withstand multiple autoclave sterilization cycles, perfect for identifying reusable sterilization containers and tracking sterilized consumables, devices, and packages. Combining printed visual information with encoded RFID data, these thermal-transfer labels are the ideal identification solution for improving the traceability of sterilized material, including for GMP and GLP operations. Designed to integrate with RFID sterilization management systems to control and monitor the sterilization process.
