# DYMO LabelWriter 550

Source: [https://www.labtag.com/solutions/dymo-labelwriter-550/](https://www.labtag.com/solutions/dymo-labelwriter-550/)

![DYMO LabelWriter 550 Cryogenic Label Printing Kit](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/PKT-LWCS-506-Box.png "/media/dymo-labelwriter-550/PKT-LWCS-506-Box.png")

The DYMO® LabelWriter™ 550 Cryogenic Label Printing Kit pairs high-quality CryoSTUCK® cryogenic labels with the DYMO LabelWriter 5-Series for laboratory identification. A free DYMO® LabelWriter™ 550 printer is available when purchasing eight rolls of CryoSTUCK® cryogenic labels—giving cryogenic labs a ready desktop printing solution for frozen vials and tubes.

DYMO® partners with GA International to deliver laboratory labeling solutions designed for the DYMO LabelWriter 5-Series desktop printers.

### By Kit

- **CryoSTUCK® Labels** — DYMO-branded cryogenic labels for LN₂, ultra-low freezers, and dry ice—apply to ambient or frozen surfaces
- **LabelWriter™ 550 Printer** — Direct-thermal, inkless printing with Automatic Label Recognition™
- **Connect™ Software** — Windows and Mac design, bulk print from Excel and databases

### By Size

- **2″ × 1″** — LWCS-501 rectangle labels for vials and tubes
- **2″ × 0.25″** — LWCS-502 narrow wrap-style labels
- **1″ × 1″** — LWCS-504 square labels for caps and vials
- **1″ × 0.5″** — LWCS-505 compact labels for small containers

## Printing Kit by Component

### CryoSTUCK® Labels

![CryoSTUCK labels with DYMO and GA branding](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/LWCS-504-DYMOxGA.jpg "/media/dymo-labelwriter-550/LWCS-504-DYMOxGA.jpg")

DYMO-branded CryoSTUCK® labels are designed for storage in liquid nitrogen, ultra-low temperature freezers, and transport on dry ice (min. −196°C/−321°F). Ideal for labeling ambient-temperature and frozen surfaces without thawing (min. −80°C/−112°F). Chemical resistance covers common lab chemicals, including incidental exposure to alcohols and solvents. Authentic DYMO-branded labels are automatically detected by LabelWriter® 5-Series printers (550, 550 Turbo, 5XL).

### LabelWriter™ 550 Printer

![DYMO LabelWriter 550 printer](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/Dymo-LabelWriter-550-Printer-2-2-1.jpg "/media/dymo-labelwriter-550/Dymo-LabelWriter-550-Printer-2-2-1.jpg")

Patented Automatic Label Recognition™ identifies label size, type, and remaining quantity. Inkless direct-thermal printing of high-quality barcodes, QR codes, images, and text. Jam-free printing with automatic label alignment, a compact benchtop footprint, and print speeds up to 62 labels per minute. Setup takes about three minutes from unboxing to the first printed label—no additional inks or software subscriptions required.

### Connect™ Software

![DYMO LabelWriter 550 printing kit](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/PKT-LWCS-506-thumb.jpg "/media/dymo-labelwriter-550/PKT-LWCS-506-thumb.jpg")

DYMO® Connect™ software works on Windows and Mac OS. Print in bulk from Microsoft Excel and databases, generate barcodes and QR codes, or add an image or shape. Integrated spellcheck reduces spelling errors. Templates for each LWCS-class label are pre-loaded—once labels are inserted, the software recognizes and loads the correct template. Compatible with SciSure and LabCollector LIMS.

## FAQ

### Is this solution right for me?

This solution is ideal if you’re looking for a simple, scalable, and budget-friendly labeling system. It caters to institutions of all sizes that need a straightforward labeling system with minimal upfront costs. As your needs grow, you can add more printers to your fleet and integrate with your other systems.

### Does the printer use an ink ribbon to print on the labels?

No, the LabelWriter™ 5 series printers and LWCS-class labels use direct-thermal technology and therefore do not require a ribbon or any other ink source to print.

### Is technical support and the software license free?

Yes, technical support for the DYMO LabelWriter 5 Series printer, labels, and software is free and the software requires no subscription.

### Is this solution compatible with Windows & macOS?

Yes, the DYMO LabelWriter 550 printer and DYMO Connect software are compatible with Windows 10 (or later) and macOS 13 (or later).

### Can I import data from Excel or databases?

Yes, the DYMO Connect software allows you to import spreadsheet or database data in the following file formats: .accdb, .mdb, .xlsx, .xlsb, .xlsm, .xls, .txt, .csv & .udl.

### Do I need to manually create templates in the software?

No, the DYMO Connect software is pre-loaded with templates for each of our LWCS-class labels. Once the labels are inserted in the printer, the software will recognize and load the correct template. There is no need to search for or create a label template.

### Is this solution compatible with LIMS?

Yes, the DYMO® LabelWriter™ 550 printer is compatible with SciSure and LabCollector.
