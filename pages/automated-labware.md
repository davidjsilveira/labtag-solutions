High-volume labs lose time and IDs to hand-applied labels.

Two ways out: run a tube or plate labeler in the room, or send labware out for print-and-apply before it arrives. HTI and Scinomix keep capping, filling, and barcoding on the bench. Pre-labeled labware is the same job done off-site—custom barcodes, placement, and grading—when you do not want a labeler on the floor.

## HTI for small and medium batches

![X-TubeProcessor Flex](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/HTI-X-TubeProcessor-FLEX.png "/media/automated-labels/HTI-X-TubeProcessor-FLEX.png")

HTI X-TubeProcessor® units are sized for small and medium batches: compact through mixed-rack and infeed-belt models so capping, filling, and barcoding stay on the bench.

- Smart — compact, optional sterile fill
- Flex — cap and tube feeder
- Sign — up to 12 mixed racks
- Basic — up to 600 tubes per hour on an infeed belt

## Scinomix when the deck has to walk away

![Sci-Print VX2 automatic tube labeler](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/Scinomix-vx2-1-1.png "/media/automated-labels/Scinomix-vx2-1-1.png")

Sci-Print VX2 and VXL share the same labeling logic; deck size is the difference. Bulk feeders, aliquoting, and plate labeling sit on the same bench family.

- Sci-Print VX2 — about 600 tubes
- Sci-Print VXL — about 1,200 tubes; optional uncap and recap
- Bulk feeders — 0.5–15 mL vials
- AQ — aliquots, pools, and dilutes into labeled tubes
- MP2+ — labels all sides of a microplate and reads the barcode

## Send the tubes out unlabeled

- Send yours — ship tubes and vials

- Print — barcodes, variable text, graphics, and logos
