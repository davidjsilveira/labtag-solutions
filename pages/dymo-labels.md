LabTAG provides laboratory labels compatible with DYMO® LabelWriter™ 5-Series and 4-Series printers for identifying vials, tubes, boxes, and other laboratory containers. Available in various sizes and configurations, these durable labels offer resistance to a wide range of temperatures and harsh chemical exposure during routine cryogenic and chemical lab identification.

Options cover cryogenic storage—including labels that adhere to already frozen surfaces—plus deep-freeze, heat-resistant, water-dissolvable, and general-use formats designed for lab and office DYMO LabelWriter printing.

## Labels for LabelWriter™ 5-Series

### CryoSTUCK® Labels

![CryoSTUCK labels for DYMO LabelWriter 5-Series](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/LWCS-504-DYMOxGA.jpg "/media/dymo-compatible-laboratory-labels/LWCS-504-DYMOxGA.jpg")

CryoSTUCK® labels for DYMO LabelWriter 5-Series printers (550, 550 Turbo, 5XL) are designed for direct application to frozen containers at −80°C without thawing. Their permanent adhesive endures prolonged liquid nitrogen storage at −196°C (−321°F), preserving sample integrity for research labs and biobanks. Temperature range is −196°C to +70°C (−321°F to +158°F). Resistant to detergents, ethanol, acetone, and xylene, they are suited to harsh lab environments.

## Labels for LabelWriter™ 4-Series

### Frozen Vials

![Cryogenic labels for DYMO LabelWriter frozen vials](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/EDCS-070-Use5.jpg "/media/dymo-compatible-laboratory-labels/EDCS-070-Use5.jpg")

DYMO-compatible, solvent-resistant cryogenic labels (EDCS-class) rapidly adhere to already frozen surfaces such as cryo vials and tubes. They can be applied under deep-freeze conditions (−80°C), eliminating the need to thaw samples beforehand, and can then be placed immediately back into freezers (−80°C) and liquid nitrogen tanks (−196°C) with no cure time required. Temperature range is −196°C to +70°C (−321°F to +158°F).

They display excellent resistance to laboratory chemicals such as alcohols (100% ethanol, immersion up to 15 minutes), xylene (immersion up to 30 minutes), detergents, and disinfecting solutions. Available in a range of sizes and colors for printing with DYMO LabelWriter 450, 450 Turbo, and 4XL printers, these cryo labels can be printed with 1D and 2D barcodes, alphanumeric text, and variable data.

### SimPEEL™ Frozen Surfaces

![SimPEEL cryogenic labels for frozen surfaces](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/SPEDCS-070WH-Use1.jpg "/media/dymo-compatible-laboratory-labels/SPEDCS-070WH-Use1.jpg")

Direct thermal printable cryogenic labels for already frozen surfaces, compatible with the 450-series and 4XL DYMO LabelWriter printers. Provided with SimPEEL™ technology, they peel quickly from their liner and apply to already frozen vials and tubes (−80°C) before storage in liquid nitrogen (−196°C) and lab freezers. Temperature range is −196°C to +70°C (−321°F to +158°F). These cryo labels also resist routinely used laboratory chemicals and surface-sanitizing wipes.

### Cryogenic Labels

![DYMO-compatible cryogenic labels on vials and microtubes](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/EC1F-072-Use.png "/media/dymo-compatible-laboratory-labels/EC1F-072-Use.png")

DYMO-compatible cryogenic labels (ED1F/EF1F/EG1F/EC1F-class) are designed for identifying cryo vials, tubes, and boxes stored under cryogenic conditions—including ultra-low temperature freezers (−80°C), liquid nitrogen (−196°C), and dry-ice transport. Temperature range is −196°C to +70°C (−321°F to +158°F). Print alphanumeric text, serialized data, and 1D/2D barcodes on LabelWriter 450-series and 4XL printers.

### SimPEEL™ Cryogenic

![Cryogenic labels with SimPEEL technology](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/SPED1F-070WH-Use2.jpg "/media/dymo-compatible-laboratory-labels/SPED1F-070WH-Use2.jpg")

DYMO-compatible cryogenic labels with SimPEEL™ technology (SPED1F-class) peel easily from their liner and apply to the intended surface. Designed to withstand long-term storage in ultra-low temperature freezers (−80°C) and liquid nitrogen (−196°C), with a temperature range of −196°C to +70°C (−321°F to +158°F). Compatible with LabelWriter 450-series and 4XL printers.

### Transparent Deep-Freeze

![Transparent deep-freeze DYMO-compatible labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/EDFT-070-Use1.jpg "/media/dymo-compatible-laboratory-labels/EDFT-070-Use1.jpg")

Freezer-grade transparent labels (EDFT-class) for containers stored in low-temperature freezers, refrigerators, cold rooms, or transported on dry ice. Temperature range is −80°C to +70°C (−112°F to +158°F). The clear format will not obscure the container’s contents. They also tolerate elevated temperatures, suitable for identifying cell culture flasks and plates stored in incubators.

### Heat-Resistant

![Heat-resistant DYMO-compatible labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/EDAP-070-use2.jpg "/media/dymo-compatible-laboratory-labels/EDAP-070-use2.jpg")

EDAP-class labels compatible with DYMO 450 and 4XL LabelWriter® printers withstand elevated temperatures in dry (ovens) and wet (water baths) conditions. Temperature range is −80°C to +95°C (−112°F to +203°F). Unlike other direct thermal labels, they will not turn entirely dark when exposed to high-heat conditions—suited to containers or devices destined for high-heat applications.

### Water Dissolvable

![Water dissolvable DYMO-compatible paper labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/EDWA-010-Use-4.jpg "/media/dymo-compatible-laboratory-labels/EDWA-010-Use-4.jpg")

EDWA-class paper labels completely disintegrate when exposed to water and simple hand or mechanical rubbing. Compatible with DYMO 450 and 4XL printers, they are suited to containers subject to frequent washing: wash them off and relabel to maintain a fresh appearance. Temperature range is −40°C to +70°C (−40°F to +158°F).

### General Use

![General use DYMO-compatible color labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/ECY-050-Use.jpg "/media/dymo-compatible-laboratory-labels/ECY-050-Use.jpg")

DYMO-compatible paper labels (EDY/ECY-class) printable with the 450-series of DYMO LabelWriter printers include alphanumeric text, serialized information, and 1D/2D barcodes for tracking and organization. Available in a variety of colors, they are suited to general use in the lab and office—folders, notebooks, boxes, paperwork, and shipping labels.

## FAQ

### Is this solution right for me?

This solution is ideal if you’re looking for a simple, scalable, and budget-friendly labeling system. It caters to institutions of all sizes that need a straightforward labeling system with minimal upfront costs. As your needs grow, you can add more printers to your fleet and integrate with your other systems.

### Does the printer use an ink ribbon to print on the labels?

No, the LabelWriter™ 5 series printers and LWCS-class labels use direct-thermal technology and therefore do not require a ribbon or any other ink source to print.

### Is technical support and the software license free?

Yes, technical support for the DYMO LabelWriter 5 Series printer, labels, and software is free and the software requires no subscription.

### Is this solution compatible with Windows & macOS?

Yes, the DYMO LabelWriter 550 printer and DYMO Connect software are compatible with Windows 10 (or later) and macOS 13 (or later).

### Can I import data from Excel or databases?

Yes, the DYMO Connect software allows you to import spreadsheet or database data in the following file formats: .accdb, .mdb, .xlsx, .xlsb, .xlsm, .xls, .txt, .csv & .udl.

### Do I need to manually create templates in the software?

No, the DYMO Connect software is pre-loaded with templates for each of our LWCS-class labels. Once the labels are inserted in the printer, the software will recognize and load the correct template. There is no need to search for or create a label template.

### Is this solution compatible with LIMS?

Yes, the DYMO® LabelWriter™ 550 printer is compatible with SciSure and LabCollector.
