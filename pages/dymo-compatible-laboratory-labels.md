## Cryogenic Labels for Frozen Vials

![A gloved hand holding a microtube labeled with a DYMO-compatible cryogenic label, next to two labeled cryo vials and a conical tube on a lab table.](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/EC1F-072-Use.png "/media/dymo-compatible-laboratory-labels/EC1F-072-Use.png")

-196°C to +70°C (-321°F to +158°F)

CryoSTUCK® labels are now available for DYMO LabelWriter 5-Series printers for direct application to frozen containers at -80°C without thawing. Their permanent adhesive endures prolonged liquid nitrogen storage at -196°C, preserving sample integrity for research labs and biobanks. Resistant to detergents, ethanol, acetone, and xylene, they’re ideal for harsh lab environments.

## Cryogenic Labels for Frozen Vials (Patented)

![Cryogenic Labels for Frozen Vials (Patented)](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/SPED1F-070WH-Use2.jpg "/media/dymo-compatible-laboratory-labels/SPED1F-070WH-Use2.jpg")

From -196°C to +70°C (-321°F to +158°F)

These DYMO-compatible, solvent-resistant cryogenic labels will rapidly adhere to already frozen surfaces, such as cryo vials and tubes. They can be applied under deep-freeze conditions (-80°C), eliminating the need to thaw samples beforehand, and can then be placed immediately back into storage inside freezers (-80°C) and liquid nitrogen tanks (-196°C) with no cure time required. They display excellent resistance to laboratory chemicals like alcohols (100% Ethanol, immersion: up to 15 minutes), xylene (immersion: up to 30 minutes), detergents, and disinfecting solutions.

## Cryogenic Labels for Frozen Surfaces with SimPEEL™ Technology (Patented)

![Cryogenic Labels for Frozen Surfaces with SimPEEL™ Technology (Patented)](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/EDFT-070-Use1.jpg "/media/dymo-compatible-laboratory-labels/EDFT-070-Use1.jpg")

From -196°C to +70°C (-321°F to +158°F)

Direct thermal printable cryogenic labels for already frozen surfaces compatible with the 450 series and 4XL DYMO LabelWriter printers. Provided with our unique SimPEEL technology, they can be easily and quickly peeled from their liner and applied to already frozen vials and tubes (-80°C), prior to storage in liquid nitrogen (-196°C) and lab freezers. These cryo labels also display excellent resistance to routinely used laboratory chemicals and surface-sanitizing wipes.

ED1F/EF1F/EG1F/EC1F-Class

From -196°C to +70°C (-321°F to +158°F)

DYMO-compatible cryogenic labels are designed for identifying cryo vials, tubes, and boxes stored under cryogenic conditions. This includes storage in ultra-low temperature freezers (-80°C), liquid nitrogen tanks (-196°C), and during transport on dry ice.

Available in a range of sizes, and colors, for printing with DYMO LabelWriter 450, 450 turbo, and 4XL printers, these cryo labels can be printed with 1D & 2D barcodes, alphanumeric text, and variable data.

## Heat-Resistant Labels (Patent Pending)

![Heat-Resistant Labels (Patent Pending)](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/EDAP-070-use2.jpg "/media/dymo-compatible-laboratory-labels/EDAP-070-use2.jpg")

From -80°C to +95°C (-112°F to +203°F)

EDAP-class labels are compatible with DYMO 450 and 4XL LabelWriter® printers and can withstand elevated temperatures in dry (ovens) and wet (water baths) conditions. Unlike other direct thermal labels, they will not turn entirely dark when exposed to high-heat conditions, perfect for labeling various containers or devices destined for high-heat applications.

EDWA-Class

Water Dissolvable Paper Labels

From -40°C to +70°C (-40°F to +158°F)

These paper labels will completely disintegrate when exposed to water and simple hand or mechanical rubbing. Compatible with DYMO 450 and 4XL printers, these water dissolvable labels are perfect for labeling containers subject to frequent washing, requiring no additional effort to remove. Simply wash them off, and relabel to maintain a fresh-looking appearance.

## General Use Labels

![General Use Labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/EDWA-010-Use-4.jpg "/media/dymo-compatible-laboratory-labels/EDWA-010-Use-4.jpg")

These DYMO-compatible paper labels can be printed with the 450-series of DYMO LabelWriter printers to include alphanumeric text, serialized information, and 1D/2D barcodes for improved tracking and organization. Available in a variety of colors, they are ideal for all general use applications, both in the lab and office, such as the identification of folders, notebooks, boxes, paperwork, and as shipping labels.
