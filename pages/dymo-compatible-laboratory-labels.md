# Dymo-Compatible Laboratory Labels

Source: [https://www.labtag.com/solutions/dymo-compatible-laboratory-labels/](https://www.labtag.com/solutions/dymo-compatible-laboratory-labels/)

![Dymo-Compatible Laboratory Labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/LWCS-501-use2.jpg "/media/dymo-compatible-laboratory-labels/LWCS-501-use2.jpg")



LabTAG provides laboratory labels compatible with DYMO® LabelWriter™ 5-Series and 4-Series printers for identifying vials, tubes, boxes, and other laboratory containers. Available in various sizes and configurations, these durable labels offer resistance to a wide range of temperatures and harsh chemical exposure during routine cryogenic and chemical lab identification.

Options cover cryogenic storage—including labels that adhere to already frozen surfaces—plus deep-freeze, heat-resistant, water-dissolvable, and general-use formats designed for lab and office DYMO LabelWriter printing.

## DYMO-Compatible Label Guide

Everything you need to know before choosing the right DYMO-compatible labels. Browse options by printer series or application below.

### By Printer Series

- **5-Series (550, 550 Turbo, 5XL)** — LWCS-class CryoSTUCK® labels for LabelWriter™ 5-Series printers
- **4-Series (450, 450 Turbo, 4XL)** — EDCS, SPEDCS, EDFT, and related classes for LabelWriter™ 4-Series printers

### By Application

- **Cryogenic / Frozen Surfaces** — Stick to frozen vials at −80°C; endure liquid nitrogen at −196°C
- **Deep-Freeze & Transparent** — Freezer, refrigerator, and incubator labels, including clear formats
- **Heat-Resistant** — Labels that stay legible in ovens and water baths
- **Water Dissolvable** — Paper labels that wash off with water and light rubbing
- **General Use** — Color paper labels for folders, notebooks, boxes, and shipping

## Labels for LabelWriter™ 5-Series

### CryoSTUCK® Labels (LWCS-Class)

![CryoSTUCK labels for DYMO LabelWriter 5-Series](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/EC1F-072-Use.png "/media/dymo-compatible-laboratory-labels/EC1F-072-Use.png")

CryoSTUCK® labels for DYMO LabelWriter 5-Series printers are designed for direct application to frozen containers at −80°C without thawing. Their permanent adhesive endures prolonged liquid nitrogen storage at −196°C (−321°F), preserving sample integrity for research labs and biobanks. Temperature range is −196°C to +70°C (−321°F to +158°F). Resistant to detergents, ethanol, acetone, and xylene, they are suited to harsh lab environments.

## Labels for LabelWriter™ 4-Series

### Cryogenic Labels for Frozen Vials

![Cryogenic labels for DYMO LabelWriter 4-Series](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/SPEDCS-070WH-Use1.jpg "/media/dymo-compatible-laboratory-labels/SPEDCS-070WH-Use1.jpg")
![Patented cryogenic labels for frozen vials](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/SPED1F-070WH-Use2.jpg "/media/dymo-compatible-laboratory-labels/SPED1F-070WH-Use2.jpg")

DYMO-compatible, solvent-resistant cryogenic labels (SPEDCS-class and related) rapidly adhere to already frozen surfaces such as cryo vials and tubes. They can be applied under deep-freeze conditions (−80°C), eliminating the need to thaw samples beforehand, and can then be placed immediately back into freezers (−80°C) and liquid nitrogen tanks (−196°C) with no cure time required. Temperature range is −196°C to +70°C (−321°F to +158°F).

They display excellent resistance to laboratory chemicals such as alcohols (100% ethanol, immersion up to 15 minutes), xylene (immersion up to 30 minutes), detergents, and disinfecting solutions. Available in a range of sizes and colors for printing with DYMO LabelWriter 450, 450 Turbo, and 4XL printers, these cryo labels can be printed with 1D and 2D barcodes, alphanumeric text, and variable data.

### Cryogenic Labels with SimPEEL™ Technology

![Cryogenic labels with SimPEEL technology](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/EDFT-070-Use1.jpg "/media/dymo-compatible-laboratory-labels/EDFT-070-Use1.jpg")

Direct thermal printable cryogenic labels for already frozen surfaces, compatible with the 450-series and 4XL DYMO LabelWriter printers. Provided with SimPEEL™ technology, they peel quickly from their liner and apply to already frozen vials and tubes (−80°C) before storage in liquid nitrogen (−196°C) and lab freezers. Temperature range is −196°C to +70°C (−321°F to +158°F). These cryo labels also resist routinely used laboratory chemicals and surface-sanitizing wipes.

### Transparent Deep-Freeze Labels

Freezer-grade transparent labels (EDAP-class) for containers stored in low-temperature freezers, refrigerators, cold rooms, or transported on dry ice. Temperature range is −80°C to +70°C (−112°F to +158°F). The clear format will not obscure the container’s contents. They also tolerate elevated temperatures, suitable for identifying cell culture flasks and plates stored in incubators.

### Heat-Resistant Labels

EDAP-class labels compatible with DYMO 450 and 4XL LabelWriter® printers withstand elevated temperatures in dry (ovens) and wet (water baths) conditions. Temperature range is −80°C to +95°C (−112°F to +203°F). Unlike other direct thermal labels, they will not turn entirely dark when exposed to high-heat conditions—suited to containers or devices destined for high-heat applications.

### Water Dissolvable Paper Labels

EDWA-class paper labels completely disintegrate when exposed to water and simple hand or mechanical rubbing. Compatible with DYMO 450 and 4XL printers, they are suited to containers subject to frequent washing: wash them off and relabel to maintain a fresh appearance. Temperature range is −40°C to +70°C (−40°F to +158°F).

### General Use Labels

![General use DYMO-compatible labels](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/EDCS-070-Use5.jpg "/media/dymo-compatible-laboratory-labels/EDCS-070-Use5.jpg")

DYMO-compatible paper labels (EDY/ECY-class) printable with the 450-series of DYMO LabelWriter printers include alphanumeric text, serialized information, and 1D/2D barcodes for tracking and organization. Available in a variety of colors, they are suited to general use in the lab and office—folders, notebooks, boxes, paperwork, and shipping labels.
