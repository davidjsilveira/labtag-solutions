# Automated Tube Labelers

Source: [https://www.labtag.com/solutions/automated-labels/](https://www.labtag.com/solutions/automated-labels/)

![Automated Tube Labelers](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/20241128_084524.jpg "/media/automated-labels/20241128_084524.jpg")



Automated labeling machines are suited to high-volume labs: they save time, reduce errors, and cut labor costs by eliminating the need for manual labeling. Whether in biobanks, clinical research, or drug development, automated label systems streamline processes such as tube and cap handling, sorting and storage, and sterile applications for demanding production environments.

Automation devices from HTI and Scinomix support consistent barcode scanning and high-throughput labeling while reducing wrinkling, peeling, and manual handling errors in the lab.

## HTI Automated Tube & Vial Handling

HTI standard devices are designed exclusively for batch operations and are also suitable for filling tasks in small and medium-sized laboratories in terms of size, throughput, and costs.

### X-TubeProcessor® Smart

![X-TubeProcessor Smart](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/HTI-X-TubeProcessor-SMART.png "/media/automated-labels/HTI-X-TubeProcessor-SMART.png")

A compact solution for labeling tubes with sterile working capability via HEPA filter. Supports a peristaltic pump and air displacement pipette for filling workflows in smaller labs.

### X-TubeProcessor® Flex

![X-TubeProcessor Flex](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/HTI-X-TubeProcessor-FLEX.png "/media/automated-labels/HTI-X-TubeProcessor-FLEX.png")

Includes a cap/tube feeder and barcode identification. Flexible process customization increases walkaway time for operators running varied tube workflows.

### X-TubeProcessor® Sign

![X-TubeProcessor Sign](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/HTI-X-TubeProcessor-SIGN.png "/media/automated-labels/HTI-X-TubeProcessor-SIGN.png")

Built for efficient and flexible data management. Labels a variety of tube types and racks, with support for 12 racks for different or identical tube types.

### X-TubeProcessor® Basic

![X-TubeProcessor Basic](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/HTI-X-TubeProcessor-BASIC.png "/media/automated-labels/HTI-X-TubeProcessor-BASIC.png")

Features an infeed belt plus automated screwing and unscrewing. Process up to 600 tubes per hour for higher-throughput batch labeling.

## Scinomix Fully-Automated Tube Handling

Scinomix offers fully automated benchtop tube and microplate labeling technology that can handle tubes of various sizes, microplates, and 2D vials. The Sci-Print VX2 and Sci-Print VXL vial labelers share similar functionality, with the only difference being the number of tubes each can hold.

### VX2 Automatic Tube Labeler

![VX2 Automatic Tube Labeler](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/Scinomix-vx2-1-1.png "/media/automated-labels/Scinomix-vx2-1-1.png")

The Sci-Print VX2 features a 6-position work deck that accommodates up to 600 tubes. It handles tubes and vials ranging from 0.5 mL to 50 mL, providing walkaway time for more critical lab tasks.

### VXL Automatic Tube Labeler

![VXL Automatic Tube Labeler](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/Scinomix-Vxl-1-1.png "/media/automated-labels/Scinomix-Vxl-1-1.png")

The Sci-Print VXL features a 12-position work deck that accommodates up to 1200 tubes, with a throughput of 375 tubes per hour. Uncapping and recapping of screw-top lids is available when a filling option is required.

### Bulk Tube Feeder Add-Ons

![Bulk Tube Feeder Add-Ons](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/Vxi-bulk-tube-feeder-1-1.png "/media/automated-labels/Vxi-bulk-tube-feeder-1-1.png")

Works with a variety of vials ranging from 0.5 mL to 15 mL. Operators can easily create, store, and change multiple tube definitions, with a throughput of 400 tubes per hour.

### AQ Aliquoting System

![AQ Aliquoting System](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/Scinomix_AQ-Aliquoting-System_Hero-Shot.png "/media/automated-labels/Scinomix_AQ-Aliquoting-System_Hero-Shot.png")

Designed to aliquot reagents or samples with labeling, validation, and sample tracking. Supported workflows include sample aliquoting, sample pooling, sample dilution, and reagent dispensing. Multiple tube definitions can be created, stored, and changed as needed.

### MP2+ Automated Microplate Labeler

![MP2+ Automated Microplate Labeler](https://cdn.labtag.com/wp-content/uploads/Microplates.jpg "/media/automated-labels/Microplates.jpg")

Labels can be applied to all sides of the plate. Removable racks support easy loading and unloading, and a barcode reader is included for validation.
